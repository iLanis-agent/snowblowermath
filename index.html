Snowblower Math

The box counts horses the engine never had, the intake bites what it bites, and the sticker throw was measured with powder that fell somewhere else.

Open the app →

The engine truth

Small-engine marketing counted horses generously for decades - the honest norm is about 32 cc per horsepower (labeled). At that norm the 208-cc blower makes about 6.5 honest horses, whatever the box says.

The intake truth

The intake height names the bite each pass takes. Storm inches divided by intake inches is the pass count - one honest pass, two passes with no shame, or shovel the top first.

The throw truth

The sticker throw was measured with dry powder on a good day. Packed snow keeps about 0.55 of it and wet snow about 0.35 (labeled norms) - the honest feet decide whether the drop zone or the hedge gets the snow.

What it never does

It never counts marketing horses, never reads the sticker throw as the wet-snow throw, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the snow on the hedge keeps the honest ledger.

Snowblower Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
